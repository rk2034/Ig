Campus Facilities

Library

The library contains technical books, general books, newspapers and magazines. It is open to staff and students with valid identity cards. Strict silence is maintained to ensure a productive learning environment.

Internet Cafe

High-speed broadband internet facility is provided to students and staff. The network operates with complete redundancy and high-throughput connectivity, enabling access to unlimited educational resources.

Transportation

College buses operate on multiple routes covering major parts of the city and surrounding areas, ensuring safe and convenient transportation for students and staff members.

Sports

The college provides excellent sports facilities open to all students, encouraging both competitive participation and recreational engagement for overall physical development.

Hostels

A well-designed hostel is situated outside the campus, surrounded by greenery. It offers single, double, and four-seater rooms with excellent amenities and hygienic food facilities.

Cafeteria

The cafeteria serves a variety of snacks and beverages on working days. Lunch facilities are also available for students, staff, and visitors in a clean and comfortable setting.

Health Center

The college provides a well-equipped health center to ensure the well-being of students and staff. Basic medical facilities and first-aid services are available on campus to address immediate health concerns.

Language Lab

The Language Lab is designed to enhance communication and soft skills through interactive learning tools and digital resources. It supports students in developing proficiency in English and other languages for academic and professional growth.

Emergency & Personal Safety

The institution ensures a safe and secure campus environment through well-defined emergency response systems, first-aid facilities, and safety protocols. Surveillance systems and dedicated personnel continuously monitor campus activities to maintain discipline and provide immediate assistance during unforeseen situations.
