Online Admission

Please read the instructions carefully before applying.

General Instructions
  • Please read the Prospectus and Eligibility Criteria before filling the application form.
  • Applicants and parents/guardians must familiarize themselves with the prospectus and abide by the rules.
  • Separate Application Forms must be submitted for each programme in Self Finance (Women) and Hostel.
  • The College does not collect capitation fee or donation for admission.
If anyone promises admission on payment of donation, report it to the Principal immediately.
Eligibility
  • A pass in the Higher Secondary Examination (10+2) (Academic / Vocational Stream) conducted by the Government of Tamil Nadu.
  • Or any other examination recognized as equivalent thereto by the Syndicate of Bharathidasan University.
  • The candidate must satisfy the subject requirements prescribed for the chosen programme.
  • Eligibility is subject to the rules and regulations framed by Bharathidasan University from time to time.
  • Admission shall be granted only after verification of original certificates and supporting documents.
