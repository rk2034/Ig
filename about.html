About Us

College Overview

Indra Ganesan College of Arts & Science is a distinguished institution established in 2019 under the aegis of the Indra Ganesan Educational & Charitable Trust, with a strong commitment to delivering quality higher education in Arts, Science, and Commerce disciplines.

The campus is located in a serene and conducive environment that fosters academic excellence, intellectual growth, and holistic development. Since its inception, the institution has been dedicated to nurturing young minds through value-based education, innovative teaching methodologies, and a learner-centric approach.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To mould students to become dedicated, committed, intellectually well-developed, socially concerned, morally upright and spiritually oriented individuals.

Mission

To inspire learning among young minds and provide ample opportunities to equip them for secure and meaningful employment.
